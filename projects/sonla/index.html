About the project

A research-driven urban design proposal for a mountain town facing increasingly severe monsoon flooding and heat stress. The project treats public space and landscape as climate infrastructure: terraced public ground, permeable paving and shaded corridors work together to absorb water, reduce surface temperature and improve everyday urban comfort.

Climate strategy

The proposal connects environmental performance with the public realm rather than treating climate response as a separate technical layer.

01 · Water

Absorb and slow

Terraced public ground and permeable surfaces are used as part of the urban water strategy, allowing rainfall to be absorbed and managed through the landscape.

02 · Heat

Shade and cool

Shaded corridors and planted public spaces reduce direct solar exposure and help lower surface temperature along everyday pedestrian routes.

03 · Public space

Climate as infrastructure

Streets, landscape and gathering spaces are designed as one continuous system, combining environmental resilience with everyday civic life.
