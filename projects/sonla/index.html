About the project

A climate-responsive urban design proposal for Son La that studies the relationship between landscape, water, mobility and public space. The project develops a connected urban system in which ecological strategies become part of everyday civic space, responding to flooding, heat and changing environmental conditions.

Site / Context + Problem

Territorial analysis and the key environmental and urban challenges.

Urban Systems

Existing structure, water, mobility, and green-blue networks combined into one urban strategy.
