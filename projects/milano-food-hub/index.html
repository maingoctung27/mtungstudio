About the project

Milan Community Food Hub combines anthropological research and architectural design to create temporary, sustainable community spaces. The project transforms a disused municipal market at Piazzale Selinunte in San Siro into a flexible hub for food-related events, workshops and community gatherings, using food as a catalyst for dialogue, cultural exchange and neighbourhood engagement.
