About the project

85 Ottanta Cinque Market reimagines the community food hub at Piazzale Selinunte in San Siro, Milan. The project brings together 85 different communities through food, everyday rituals and shared public space. A linear market landscape connects stalls, gathering areas, greenery and civic spaces, turning food into a catalyst for cultural exchange and neighbourhood interaction.

Project development

From metropolitan and site context to landscape strategy, the 85 concept, modular scaffolding, material system, rainwater collection and the final market atmosphere.
