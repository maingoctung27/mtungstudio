About the project

Milano Community Food Hub reimagines the former municipal market at Piazzale Selinunte in San Siro as a flexible community space where food becomes a catalyst for social connection. Developed around the idea of “OttantaCinque,” the project responds to the neighbourhood’s cultural diversity through a system of adaptable market stalls, shared spaces, landscape interventions and climate-responsive strategies, transforming an underused market into a place for gathering, cooking, exchange and everyday community life.
