Hanoi Museum Exhibition

Location
Hanoi, Vietnam
Year
2023
Type
Exhibition · Installation · Public Space

About the project

An exhibition and installation proposal for Hanoi Museum, developed through a temporary spatial framework of scaffolding, red textile elements, public circulation and sculptural interventions. The sequence moves between the museum, garden and exhibition grounds, using movement and visual thresholds to shape the visitor experience.

Publication

The project is also documented in the referenced article “Blend: Sculpture & Light”.
